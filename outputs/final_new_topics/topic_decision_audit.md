# Topic decision audit

Final list: **493 distinct additions** (128 P1; 365 P2), compared with the 1,643-topic catalog dated October 28, 2026.

All 481 topic or group headings and the six final pulmonary bullet paragraphs were covered. Grouped paragraphs were expanded. The review produced 648 decision records; these are proposal mentions, not a count of unique new pages.

The publication outcome is the final decision. Different source mentions can point to the same new page. A merge requires preserving the stated disease distinction and, where specified, broadening the existing title.

References are selected checks, not an evidence dossier for every entry. Only references positively checked for topical relevance are retained here. A literature citation does not establish that its images can be reused.

## Inclusion rules

- A distinct disease, syndrome, injury, or structural diagnosis with a useful clinical or radiologic image.
- Clinical photographs, dermoscopy, fundus photographs, endoscopy, and radiology including ultrasound and echocardiography qualify.
- Histology, organism microscopy, laboratory tests, ECG, or genetic findings alone do not qualify.
- A typical appearance can be useful without being pathognomonic. The clinical context and confirmatory diagnosis remain essential.
- Keep subtypes and sites in one parent when they share the same visual teaching content. Retain clearly different disease entities when they add meaningful visual value.

## Catalog corrections and required title expansions

- Remove “angiosarcoma of bone” from the Adamantinoma synonym list. They are different tumors. Bone angiosarcoma is deferred for independent image review; the incorrect alias does not make it an existing topic.
- Broaden Hypertrophic Obstructive Cardiomyopathy to Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy before adding apical nonobstructive images.
- Broaden Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy to Dystrophinopathies before adding Becker disease.
- Broaden Alpha-Thalassemia beyond the current HbH label before adding Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.
- Reorganize the MALT and ocular lymphoma content carefully: gastric MALT belongs to extranodal marginal zone lymphoma, while conjunctival lymphoma can include other histologies. Do not equate all conjunctival lymphomas with MALT.
- Broaden the existing pleural empyema necessitans topic to Pleural Empyema, with necessitans as a labeled complication.
- Broaden localized tracheobronchial amyloidosis to Localized Pulmonary Amyloidosis before adding nodular lung disease.
- Broaden Isthmic Spondylolisthesis to Spondylolisthesis before adding the degenerative subtype; preserve pars-defect and intact-pars distinctions.
- Preserve copper deficiency and nitrous oxide as different causes of dorsal-column myelopathy when expanding the existing subacute combined degeneration page.

- Broaden Urachal Carcinoma to Urachal Anomalies and Carcinoma before adding benign cysts, with benign and malignant entities clearly separated.
