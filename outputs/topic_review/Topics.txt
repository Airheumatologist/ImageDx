Parkinson's Disease (parkinson_disease)
Subspecialty: Movement Disorders
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Hypomimia ("masked facies"), camptocormic posture, unilateral pill-rolling resting tremor, and striatal hand deformity; loss of the substantia nigra Nigrosome-1 ("absent swallow-tail sign") on 3T/7T SWI MRI and asymmetric "comma-to-period" loss on DaTscan SPECT.
Cervical Dystonia (Spasmodic Torticollis) (cervical_dystonia)
Subspecialty: Movement Disorders
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Sustained involuntary patterned head rotation/tilt with visible rope-like sternocleidomastoid hypertrophy and the classic bedside sensory trick (geste antagoniste); asymmetric muscle hypertrophy and hypermetabolism on neck CT/MRI and FDG-PET.
Hemifacial Spasm (hemifacial_spasm)
Subspecialty: Cranial Neuropathies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Paroxysmal unilateral orbicularis oculi closure with simultaneous ipsilateral eyebrow elevation and corner-of-mouth traction ("other Babinski sign" / Babinski-2 sign); AICA/PICA vascular loop compressing the CN VII root exit zone on 3D CISS/FIESTA MRI.
Marcus Gunn Jaw-Winking Syndrome (marcus_gunn_jaw_winking_syndrome)
Subspecialty: Cranial Neuropathies & Congenital Cranial Dysinnervation Disorders
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Unilateral congenital upper-eyelid ptosis at rest with rapid upward eyelid retraction ("wink") upon jaw opening or lateral pterygoid contraction (trigemino-oculomotor synkinesis).
Adie Tonic Pupil (Holmes-Adie Syndrome) (adie_tonic_pupil_syndrome)
Subspecialty: Neuro-Ophthalmology & Pupillary Disorders
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Unilateral dilated pupil with light-near dissociation, sectoral iris sphincter palsy with "vermiform" writhing on slit-lamp exam, and dramatic constriction to dilute 0.125% pilocarpine.
Ramsay Hunt Syndrome (Herpes Zoster Oticus) (ramsay_hunt_syndrome)
Subspecialty: Cranial Neuropathies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Acute ipsilateral lower motor neuron facial paralysis (lagophthalmos, Bell phenomenon, effaced nasolabial fold) paired with painful erythematous vesicles in the auricular concha/external auditory canal; linear gadolinium enhancement of the geniculate ganglion and CN VII/VIII on temporal bone MRI.
Myotonia Congenita (myotonia_congenita)
Subspecialty: Neuromuscular & Myotonic Disorders
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Generalized skeletal muscle hypertrophy ("Herculean" physique), delayed hand-grip and eyelid relaxation ("warm-up phenomenon"), and visible percussion myotonia dimpling of the thenar eminence/tongue; diffuse muscle hypertrophy without fatty replacement on muscle MRI.
Nemaline Myopathy (nemaline_myopathy)
Subspecialty: Congenital Myopathies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Elongated expressionless myopathic facies, high-arched palate, tented "inverted-V" upper lip, and infantile hypotonia; pathognomonic dark red/purple rod-like nemaline bodies on modified Gomori trichrome muscle biopsy.
Ullrich Congenital Muscular Dystrophy (ullrich_congenital_muscular_dystrophy)
Subspecialty: Muscular Dystrophies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Severe neonatal hypotonia, extreme distal joint hyperlaxity (hyperextensible fingers/wrists/toes with protruding calcanei) paired with proximal contractures, follicular hyperkeratosis, and keloids; concentric outer-rim fatty infiltration of the vastus lateralis ("tiger-like" / "target" sign) on thigh MRI.
Bethlem Myopathy (bethlem_myopathy)
Subspecialty: Muscular Dystrophies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Childhood/adult-onset limb-girdle weakness with pathognomonic long-finger flexion contractures on passive wrist extension ("Bethlem prayer sign"), elbow/Achilles contractures, cigarette-paper/keloid scars, and central "shadow/target" fatty band around the rectus femoris central aponeurosis on thigh MRI.
Chronic Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyradiculoneuropathy (CIDP) (chronic_inflammatory_demyelinating_polyradiculoneuropathy)
Subspecialty: Autoimmune Neuropathies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Symmetric proximal and distal weakness with intrinsic hand/foot muscle wasting; dramatic fusiform hypertrophy and STIR hyperintensity/enhancement of the brachial plexus, lumbosacral plexus, and cauda equina on MRI, with "onion-bulb" formations on sural nerve biopsy.
Subacute Combined Degeneration of the Spinal Cord (subacute_combined_degeneration)
Subspecialty: Metabolic & Nutritional Myelopathies
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Spastic-ataxic gait, beefy-red atrophic Hunter glossitis, and knuckle hyperpigmentation; pathognomonic symmetric dorsal column T2 hyperintensity ("inverted V sign" / "rabbit ears") on axial cervical/thoracic spine MRI.
Dandy-Walker Malformation (dandy_walker_malformation)
Subspecialty: Neurodevelopmental & Posterior Fossa Malformations
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Infantile macrocephaly with prominent occipital bossing; sagittal brain MRI triad of cerebellar vermian hypoplasia/upward rotation, cystic 4th ventricular dilatation filling an enlarged posterior fossa, and torcular-lambdoid inversion.
Schizencephaly (schizencephaly)
Subspecialty: Neurodevelopmental & Cortical Malformations

Encephalocraniocutaneous Lipomatosis (Haberland Syndrome) (encephalocraniocutaneous_lipomatosis)
Subspecialty: Neurocutaneous Syndromes & Phakomatoses
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Unilateral hairless fatty scalp plaque (nevus psiloliparus), ipsilateral pink epibulbar lipodermoid choristoma, periocular skin tags, and ipsilateral T1-bright intracranial/intraspinal leptomeningeal lipomas on brain/spine MRI.
Cobb Syndrome (Spinal Arteriovenous Metameric Syndrome) (cobb_syndrome)
Subspecialty: Neurocutaneous & Neurovascular Syndromes
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Well-demarcated cutaneous port-wine stain, angioma, or angiokeratoma in a specific trunk/limb dermatome paired with an intramedullary or paraspinal arteriovenous malformation at the corresponding spinal cord metamere on spinal MRI and angiography.

Pineal Germinoma (pineal_germinoma)
Subspecialty: CNS Oncology & Neuro-Ophthalmology
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Parinaud dorsal midbrain syndrome (supranuclear upgaze paralysis, Collier "tucked-lid" retraction, convergence-retraction nystagmus, light-near dissociation); midline pineal mass "engulfing" a central pineal calcification on CT with vivid enhancement and DWI restriction on MRI.
Glioblastoma (glioblastoma)
Subspecialty: CNS Oncology
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Focal deficits and florid papilledema; thick, shaggy ring-enhancing intra-axial mass with central necrosis, extensive vasogenic edema, and corpus callosum invasion ("butterfly glioma") on brain MRI, with pseudopalisading necrosis and glomeruloid microvascular proliferation on histology.
Intracranial Meningioma (intracranial_meningioma)
Subspecialty: CNS Oncology
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Proptosis, cranial nerve palsies, or Foster Kennedy syndrome (ipsilateral optic atrophy + contralateral papilledema in olfactory groove meningioma); extra-axial mass with homogeneous enhancement, CSF cleft, dural tail, adjacent calvarial hyperostosis, and "sunburst" vascular pedicle on MRI/CT, plus meningothelial whorls and psammoma bodies on pathology.
Pilocytic Astrocytoma (pilocytic_astrocytoma)
Subspecialty: CNS Oncology
Clinical / Imaging Findings: Pediatric cerebellar ataxia, head tilt, or proptosis/optic atrophy (in NF1 optic pathway glioma); classic posterior fossa cyst with an avidly enhancing mural nodule on brain MRI and bipolar hair-like ("pilocytic") cells with eosinophilic Rosenthal fibers on pathology.
Hypothalamic Hamartoma (hypothalamic_hamartoma)

• Drug-induced gingival enlargement — mulberry gingiva from phenytoin, cyclosporine, or a calcium-channel blocker.

• Molar-incisor hypomineralization — chalky yellow-brown opacities limited to first permanent molars and incisors.

• Retropharyngeal abscess — a widened prevertebral space and a rim-enhancing collection.
